Alley Prototype

One camera with a voice. Beeps begin when someone appears far away and speed up as they come closer. If someone stays close, or people gather, it speaks.

Tap Start, then turn the phone sideways.

Range: tune the beeps to this alley

Have someone stand at the far end and tap Set far. Then have them stand where beeping should be fastest and tap Set close. Both buttons are on the bar at the bottom of the full-screen camera view.

Below 1: speeds up early. Above 1: stays slow, then rushes at the end.
